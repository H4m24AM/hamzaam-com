WHO I AM

I’m Hamza.AM, a Brooklyn native whose interests and experiences have taken me well beyond the borough. I’ve worked across education, technology, real estate, photography, and business. I’ve lived abroad, written two books, and spent much of my life studying people, cultures, and ideas. That curiosity still shapes how I work and what I choose to make.

In Brooklyn real estate, I help people navigate sales, rentals, and investment. Every property has details that matter to the person considering it. A floor plan can make the layout clear. Good photographs can show how the rooms connect and where the light falls. Through Listing Photos, I offer property photography and floor plans for agents and owners who want to show those details clearly.

As a Brooklyn photographer, I’m drawn to the way people inhabit places. Black Fathers Matter examines fatherhood, presence, and relationships across generations. Faith in Brooklyn documents encounters with faith in public and communal life, and my work in Cuba looks at ordinary moments and the details that give a place its character. You can explore these projects and the architecture work through my photography portfolio.

On Tech.Hamza.AM, I share practical tools for tasks such as redacting documents and working through mortgage and property calculations. I’m interested in how a small tool can make a routine task easier to understand. My work in education informs that approach. If someone can see what a tool does and how to use it, they can decide where it fits into their own work.

Statements by Hamza.AM is where I explore culture and identity through apparel. Writing gives me another way to reflect on those subjects. In Transcending My Brooklyn, I write about living and teaching abroad, and about the perspective that comes from looking at a familiar place after spending time somewhere else.

Life is never just one thing. Neither am I.

📍 Brooklyn, NY
